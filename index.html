// about
Who I am
Background, interests, and where to find me

I am an M.Tech researcher in Data Engineering at IIIT Allahabad, working at the intersection of knowledge representation, large language models, and retrieval-augmented generation.

My research spans two directions: biomedical knowledge graph construction using ontology-guided multi-LLM pipelines with uncertainty quantification, and cross-domain philosophical-AI mapping — the first formal ontology connecting Indian epistemology (Nyaya, Mimamsa, Vedanta) to modern AI concepts.

I build systems end-to-end: from OWL 2 ontology design and SPARQL-backed retrieval to LoRA fine-tuning at 14B scale and public deployment. I am currently looking for paid remote research internships.
